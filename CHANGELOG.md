## Versioning policy

- `0.y.z` releases may change behavior while the v1.0 contract is still being validated.
- The `z` component is for backward-compatible fixes and documentation.
- The `y` component is for backward-compatible features or meaningful operational changes.
- `1.0.0` will be created only after the documented printer, accessibility, security, and deployment evidence is complete.
- Every release must be created from a reviewed, passing commit on `main` and include operator-facing notes.
